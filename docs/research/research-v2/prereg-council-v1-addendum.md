# Addendum to prereg council-v1: ambiguities resolved before the first scored run

Dated 2026-11-06, written and committed by builder CB3 BEFORE any development-window result existed. It changes no threshold, window, variant, gate or seed of `docs/prereg-council-v1.md` (tag `prereg-council-v1`); it only fixes how the frozen text is implemented where it was ambiguous. Each item states the choice and the reason. If any item is later found wrong, the fix is a new dated addendum and the affected trials are counted again as new trials (a new OPEN ledger entry).

Plumbing checks (unit tests, a smoke window on warm-up data 2013-2014 equity and 2022-02 crypto, the lookahead harness and the determinism re-run) were run before this commit only on data that is never scored; none of their numbers is used or reported as a result.

## A. Books and costs

1. **Drawdown kill switch OFF in every scored run** (filtered, unfiltered, ablations, random-veto control). The prereg says "`RiskLimits` defaults" in a list that names only position cap, risk per trade and notional cap; `docs/validation-protocol.md` v3 (kill-switch interaction, rule 1) says edge assessments run with the switch off because a latched 10% switch ends a base book after a handful of trades and makes the comparison vacuous. All other defaults stay (2% daily loss, 0.5% risk, 20% notional, 5 positions).
2. **Momentum rotation uses 10 open positions** (the strategy holds its top 10; a cap of 5 would silently turn it into a different strategy). Same as backtest-04. The other three bases use 5.
3. **Unfiltered baseline** runs through the same wired gate (`check_ctx`, lock book, ADV collar) as the filtered runs; the only difference is the entry filter. It is not the unwired `gate.check` path.
4. Equity: adjusted prices (`data/daily-pit`), adopted slippage model with `adv_cap_pct` 1 and no tick rounding (as `tq backtest` defaults), Zerodha delivery costs, `--calendar nse --settlement collateral:80`, priority Alphabetical (the 20-seed random-priority sensitivity is not part of the 48 trials and is not run here). Crypto: 1-minute BTCUSDT, flat 5 bps slippage, 0.10% taker fee per side, no calendar, 10,000 USDT. Crypto prices are divided by 10,000 (volumes multiplied) so the engine's whole-unit sizing can trade a 10,000 USDT account; returns and percentage costs are unchanged. The 1% TDS is a cash-flow timing item and is not modelled.

## B. Shadow firings (Time Machine and Exit Planner history)

5. A firing is every signal the base strategy emits, replayed in a **fresh, unconstrained account** (kill switch off, daily-loss limit lifted, no calendar gating): one run per symbol for Donchian and trend-pullback, one unconstrained rotation book for momentum rotation (every top-10 selection is a firing; partial sells of one holding are merged into one firing with the quantity-weighted exit price), one run for crypto. Shadows start at the warm-up start (equity 2012-01-01, crypto 2022-01-01) and run to the window end; point-in-time visibility is enforced only by the `exit_ts <= as_of - embargo` cut (embargo 31 calendar days equity, 7 days crypto; analog window 1,825 / 365 days), as decided by CB2.
6. Firing fields: entry price = the engine's filled (slipped) entry; exit price = the frictionless exit (`entry + (gross P&L + slippage) / qty`), so slippage is charged once, by the net-outcome model; stop distance = entry minus the strategy's stop (momentum rotation: 2 x ATR(20) at the signal bar); MFE = highest bar high from the entry bar to the exit bar minus the entry price.
7. **Time Machine bucket** = number of market flags (0, 1, 2+) computed WITHOUT the own-book flag, for firings and for decisions alike (a past firing has no book state). A firing whose flags cannot be computed (index or funding history too short) gets an unknown bucket: it counts toward the strategy-wide evidence (n >= 60) but never toward an analog bucket (n >= 30). Crypto therefore has no bucketed history before the Market voter's data exist (385 daily closes: about 2023-01-21) and abstains for most of the year; this follows from the prereg's data and is not tuned.
8. The candidate set of the scored run is exactly the engine's entry signals on symbols not already held or pending (a re-signal on a held symbol is not a candidate). Rebalancer candidates are the new-position buys of each rebalance; top-ups, trims and exits are never filtered.

## C. Council inputs and timing

9. A candidate is decided at the **signal-bar close** for the whole day's batch; the fill is the next open. The frequency caps use entries already let through at signal time (a later gate or cash rejection still consumes a slot: conservative). Priority inside a batch is Time Machine confidence, then symbol, as `Council::evaluate` does.
10. **Indicators** (point-in-time, from bars up to and including the signal bar): ATR(n) is the simple mean of the last n true ranges; trailing 20-bar statistics (median volume, ADV) include the signal bar; SMA(10); the Budget ATR% median is over the last 252 samples of ATR(14)/close, one per bar (equity) or one per 1,440 bars (crypto); crypto ADV is the mean volume of the 20 completed UTC days before the signal day. Next-session tradability uses the NSE calendar (an unanswerable date is an ABSTAIN); crypto is always tradable.
11. **Proposed weight** (Budget and Entry Timer) is the size the engine would trade without the council: risk-sized quantity at 0.5% capped by the 20% notional limit (rebalancer: the shortfall quantity to target). The Budget multiplier is `min(v, budget qty / proposed qty)` and shrinks the engine quantity at the fill (whole units, rounded down, never up). Exit Planner K is round-trip costs plus 2 x 5 bps slippage per share.
12. **Market voter book state**: own-book drawdown = 1 - equity / peak equity of the engine account; the 24-hour cooldown is not modelled.
13. **Risk voter in backtests** (`BacktestRiskVoter`): a dry run of the same `RiskGate` check the real order meets next (`check_ctx`, or the rebalancer's buy check), on a cloned limiter, with the kill-switch state taken from the backtest account instead of a persisted file (a file-backed kill switch cannot be activated in a backtest without the human acknowledgement the safety layer reserves). It can never approve what the gate rejects.

## D. Evaluation

14. **Walk-forward folds**: no parameter is fitted per fold, so the folds are the calendar test blocks of the scored window (10 half-years 2015-2019; 4 quarters of 2023) sliced from one continuous run. Purge and embargo are enforced where they matter: inside the Time Machine's point-in-time cut (embargo 31 / 7 days, as CB2 resolved), not by dropping calendar days.
15. **Tax** (book level, identical for every book and the index): equity per financial year, short-term gains (held under 365 days) at 15.6%, long-term at 10.4% above Rs 1,00,000 from FY2018-19 (exempt before), gains and losses netted inside each bucket, no loss carry-forward and no cross-bucket set-off, paid on 31 March (the last point of the window for the unfinished final year); crypto 31.2% on each realised gain at its exit, no loss offset. Trade P&L is the engine's net P&L (already after costs and slippage).
16. **Index benchmarks**: NIFTYBEES held from the last close before the first scored day to the last close of the window, Zerodha delivery costs on entry and exit, tax as if liquidated on the last day; BTC spot held from the previous UTC day's close, 0.10% fee each side, 31.2% on the terminal gain. Daily returns of the filtered, unfiltered and index books share the same first invested day.
17. **Sharpe** is annualised on 252 (equity, rf 6%) or 365 (crypto, rf 0) days of daily returns after tax; a zero-variance series has Sharpe 0. Calmar = CAGR / max drawdown of the compounded daily net series. The cell returns recorded in the ledger are those daily net returns.
18. **DSR (G2)** uses the daily net returns without subtracting the risk-free rate (as the honesty layer's cell Sharpes), `N_b` from prereg section 6, and the cross-trial Sharpe variance recorded when the sweep closes, floored at 1/T. The DSR at the ledger's own N is also reported.
19. **G1**: independent trades = distinct entry days of the council under test; entry months (equity >= 24) or entry weeks (crypto >= 12) are counted from entry dates; the abstention share is the share of candidates whose outcome is ABSTAIN. G1 is judged on the `minimal` council, or on the `full` council when no voter is retained (there is then no minimal council to count). **Precedence of outcomes**: G1 fails gives INCONCLUSIVE-SAMPLE first (a sample too small to ablate cannot show "no value added"); otherwise "no voter retained" gives KILL; otherwise GO-DEV needs G2 to G8 and G10. G9 is reported and never changes a verdict.
20. **G3**: the paired bootstrap resamples whole calendar months of the aligned daily returns of both books (10,000 draws, seed 20261106); the CI lower bound is the 2.5th percentile of the annualised Sharpe difference at the cash rate.
21. **G6**: the random-veto control drops each candidate reaching the filter with probability `1 - entries(council) / entries(unfiltered)` (the same number of entries in expectation), 1,000 runs, run seeds `cell_seed(20261107, i)`. It is run only for a base whose G1 passes (otherwise the verdict is INCONCLUSIVE-SAMPLE whatever the control would show).
22. **G7**: calibration uses every candidate of the council under test that has a Time Machine confidence and a resolved shadow outcome; the outcome is the net-of-cost, net-of-tax return of the shadow firing (`tq_analogs::outcome::net_outcome`); confidence `c` = Time Machine confidence x Market/News multiplier (1 when that voter is absent); the council's PASS set is outcome PASS or SHRINK, its VETO set is outcome VETO (ABSTAIN is excluded from both). Cold-history candidates have no confidence and are counted and excluded. Pooled equity calibration is reported, not gated.
23. **G8**: "no single fold supplies more than 50% of the total advantage" means the largest per-fold difference of compounded returns is at most half of the sum of the differences, and the sum is positive.
24. **G9** is reported only: HAC t of the daily net returns (protocol lag) against 3.0 and Sharpe at rf 0 against 0.8; the 4c-rel alpha regression and the invested-share routing are not computed.
25. **G10**: look-ahead check = `tq bias lookahead --strategy council-voters` (prefix invariance of every vote, multiplier and Time Machine confidence, built through the production adapters on real daily bars); determinism = the full council of each base run twice on the smoke window with the same seed giving byte-identical decision logs; seals = every decision log re-read, its hash chain verified and its final SEAL equal to the recorded root.

## E. Ledger

26. The committed council-v1 sweep (OPEN, N = 48) is CLOSED with the 48 cell series by `close_open_sweep`; no sweep is opened. If that sweep is already closed when the study starts, a NEW sweep with the same manifest is opened first and its 48 trials are counted.
27. Smoke and single-cell runs are not recorded in the ledger and are never reported as results.

## F. Counterfactual outcomes of re-signalled candidates (added 2026-11-06, before the first scored run)

28. Found on the smoke window (warm-up data, not scored): a vetoed breakout leaves the base strategy flat, so it signals again on every later bar while the condition holds, producing several council candidates per shadow trade (1,232 candidates against 309 shadow firings in 2013-2014). The shadow generator is position-consistent (one firing per unconstrained trade), so those later signals have no firing with their own signal bar. **Rule**: the counterfactual outcome of a candidate is the net outcome of the shadow firing with the same signal bar if one exists, otherwise of the shadow trade episode of the same symbol that covers the candidate's signal bar (`entry_ts <= signal_ts < exit_ts`); a candidate no shadow trade covers has no outcome and is counted and excluded from calibration. This changes no Time Machine input (analogs are still one firing per shadow trade) and only affects the G7 counterfactual outcomes and their counts.

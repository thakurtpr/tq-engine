## 2. Hypothesis and null
- H1: Large caps with the highest return over the formation window "t-252 to t-21 trading days" keep outperforming the rest over the next month, because of investor under-reaction and slow information diffusion (Jegadeesh and Titman 1993, "Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers", J. Finance 48(1); the one-month skip avoids short-term reversal, Jegadeesh 1990). A long-only top-bucket (the winners leg only) held equal weight and rebalanced monthly therefore earns, net of Indian delivery costs, slippage and tax, a positive return that beats NIFTYBEES, BTC and the 50/50 blend (OG2) after deflating for N_true.
- Null: net of costs and tax the top-k winners are no better than buy-and-hold; any development winner among the 27 cells (and the 364-cell lineage) is selection. Known weakness: the long-only winners leg carries the momentum crash risk without the short leg's hedge; 25-50 stocks make the top bucket concentrated and highly correlated (small N_eff); the prior identical cell was a FAIL; the universe is survivor-biased upward (section 3).

## 4. Signal, sizing, execution (detailed enough to re-implement)
- **Calendar.** Decision date t = the last trading day of each calendar month in the book's calendar; data up to the close of t only. Targets fill at the next trading day's open (the rebalancing engine path).
- **Score (all formulas).** For a symbol with daily total-return closes C(0..n), n = the index of t: `score = C(n - s) / C(n - L) - 1` (a return over the window from L days ago to s days ago; with L = 252 and s = 21 this is the classic "12-1" return of Jegadeesh-Titman: the most recent s days are skipped). Parameters: L = lookback in trading days, s = skip in trading days, k = number of names held. Eligible to be ranked on t: PIT member with a bar on t, at least max(L + 1, 253) closes of history, and `C(n - L) > 0`. No other filter (no absolute-momentum or trend filter, no volatility scaling: not in the master).
- **Selection and weights.** Rank eligible symbols by score descending, ties broken by ticker ascending (deterministic). Hold the top k at **equal weight 1/k**. If fewer than k symbols are eligible, hold those at 1/k each and the rest in cash (cash earns 0). Everything held but not selected is sold. No per-instrument parameter, one rule and one parameter vector for every instrument.
- **Sizing (fixed here; a drawdown-only failure is KILL, never a resize).** Weight-based: the rebalancing engine sizes every order from the target weight on equity at the open less a 0.5% cash buffer, so the per-trade risk percentage does not apply and the rule sets no stops (a limit of the engine's rebalance path). Max open positions **15** (= largest k), notional cap per position **20%** of equity (k = 5 gives 19.9% after the buffer, within the cap), gross <= 1 (no leverage). Monthly drift is trimmed or topped up back to 1/k when the move is >= 0.5% of equity (engine rule); smaller moves are skipped.
- **Kill-switch limit: ASSUMPTION 30%** drawdown (master 2.1; the owner may only tighten). OFF in the assessment run (limit 100%), ON in the reported operating run.
- Priority rule among simultaneous buys: alphabetical in the base run; >= 20 random-priority seeds (`--priority-seeds 20`), median judged, 5th percentile net CAGR > 0 (V1). Priority only matters when cash binds.
- Declared 4c type: **benchmark-relative** (the invested-share rule applies: a top-k long-only equity book is near fully invested, invested share >= 50% and beta >= 0.5 are expected); alpha t at 3.0 against NIFTYBEES (B1); IG1 benchmark B1. This is the stricter reading and is declared before any result.
- Pooling declaration: one rule, one parameter vector for every instrument, no per-instrument tuning: **yes**.

## 10. Assumptions and master ambiguities (listed, none moves a threshold)
1. **Return convention.** "L-day return skipping s days" is read as `C(n-s)/C(n-L) - 1`, identical to the prior momentum-rotation cell (so the base cell is the same cell). The alternative reading (window of L days ending s days ago, i.e. `C(n-s)/C(n-s-L) - 1`) was not chosen.
2. **History requirement.** At least max(L + 1, 253) closes; the 252-day part comes from master 2.5 (eligible symbols) and keeps the eligible set the same across cells; L = 189 cells could rank earlier otherwise.
3. **Fewer than k eligible**: hold the eligible names at 1/k each, rest in cash (not renormalised, no leverage).
4. **max_hold_days = 21** (trading days between decisions) for the purge gap: a held name can stay for years, so the monthly rebalance interval is the only definable hold; the purge is 21 trading days (master 3.2 monthly).
5. **Declared benchmark-relative** (4c-rel and IG1 on B1), the stricter reading.
6. Trims and top-ups: the engine skips moves under 0.5% of equity and keeps a 0.5% cash buffer; these are engine constants, not tuned.
7. **Master 2.5 vs engine trade record**: the rebalancing engine writes one trade per sale including partial trims; handled by the stricter flat-to-flat count (section 9).
8. Sizing: master's "risk per trade 0.5%" has no meaning for a weight-based rotation without stops; positions are sized by weight 1/k, with the 20% notional cap and the 15-position maximum.

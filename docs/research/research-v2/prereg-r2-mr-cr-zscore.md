## 2. Hypothesis and null
- H1: A close more than 2 standard deviations below its own 20-day mean, inside a long-term uptrend, is followed by a partial reversion toward the mean within days (short-term reversal; Jegadeesh 1990; Lehmann 1990; z-score trigger as in Avellaneda and Lee 2010). Mean reversion is long-only here (the engine and the Indian cash market have no short leg), so only the oversold side is traded.
- Null: The net-of-cost, net-of-tax mean return per trade of the rule is zero or negative; any development winner is selection among the counted cells. Known weakness: short-horizon reversals are the strongest at the shortest horizons and in the least liquid names, exactly where the cost per trade is the largest; the edge, if any, may be smaller than the 40 bps all-in round-trip cost plus tax. The trade is also a bet against momentum in a crash (a stock that keeps falling); the fixed ATR stop and the maximum hold are the only protection.

## 4. Signal, sizing, execution (detailed enough to re-implement)
- Signal definition (data up to the bar close only; every statistic uses the bar's own close and earlier bars of the SAME symbol):
  - Trend filter (fixed, not a grid axis): `C_t > SMA200_t`, the symbol's OWN 200-bar simple average of closes including `C_t` (the master says "above SMA200 filter fixed" without naming the series; the instrument's own is the conservative reading and the trend-pullback lineage's).
  - Trigger: `z_t = (C_t - m_t) / s_t` with `m_t` the mean and `s_t` the **population** standard deviation (divisor 20) of the last 20 daily closes INCLUDING `C_t` (price levels, not returns). **Signal: `z_t < z`** (strict; evaluated without a square root as `m_t - C_t > 0` and `(m_t - C_t)^2 > z^2 * s_t^2`). `s_t = 0` gives no signal.
  - Entry: flat in the symbol, filter true, trigger true, all indicators warm (>= 200 bars of the symbol's history, which also covers ATR(14) and the 20-bar window): a long entry is proposed at the signal bar's close and fills at **the next bar's open**. Crypto only: the symbol must be in the current month's top-12 set (section 3).
  - Exit (fixed): the position is closed at the next bar's open after the first bar close that is **above SMA(5)** (simple, 5 closes including that close), or after **`hold` bars** have closed since the entry fill (the entry-fill bar counts as bar 1), whichever comes first; a stop exit fills when hit (engine path, 2x slippage on stop exits for India). Holding limits: `hold` in {3, 5, 10} bars; the longest is 10 (so the spec's `max_hold_days` is 10).
  - Disaster stop (not in the master; see assumption A1): entry close minus 2 x ATR(14) (Wilder, first true range = high - low), fixed for all cells and symbols. The engine requires a stop on every order and sizes risk from it; the stop is wide relative to the 3-10 day hold so it binds only in crashes.
  - A re-entry is possible after any exit when the signal fires again. An entry the risk gate refuses leaves the rule flat and it simply re-evaluates on the next bar.
- Sizing (fixed here; **a drawdown-only failure is KILL, never a resize**): risk per trade 0.5% of equity (quantity from entry minus stop), notional cap 20% of equity per position, **max open positions 10** (<= 20; ASSUMPTION A2), gross cap <= 1 (cash only, no leverage, no short), one shared-capital account.
- **Kill-switch limit (ASSUMPTION 30%, the master's, not owner-confirmed): 30% drawdown.**
- Priority rule among simultaneous signals: base run = alphabetical by symbol (engine default); the random-priority sensitivity uses **>= 20 seeds** (the harness default 20, derived from the candidate seed 20261212); the median is judged and the 5th percentile net CAGR must be > 0 (V1).
- Declared 4c type: **absolute** (not benchmark-relative; the invested-share rule OG6 applies: invested share >= 20% of days).
- Pooling declaration: one rule, one parameter vector for every instrument, no per-instrument tuning: yes. No argmax: the base cell is the literature cell below; every other cell is a counted neighbour.

## 7. Costs, slippage, tax
- Crypto spot: taker 10 bps per side (`CryptoSpotFees` taker schedule) plus the slippage above: **about 40 bps all-in per round trip before tax** (10 + 10 taker and slippage entry, same on exit). Short-horizon mean reversion trades often, so this cost is paid on every 3-10 day trade; a gross edge below the all-in cost is a KILL, and the gating series is the net one. The doubled-cost rule for dates before 2017-08-17 does not apply (no proxy-era data enter this book). Slippage in this book: 10 bps per side adverse slippage (the engine takes one flat value per run: the conservative top-12 figure of master 7.2, 5 bps BTC/ETH and 10 bps other top-12 pairs; applying 10 to BTC/ETH too is the conservative choice). Stress: 1x/2x/3x slippage and 2x brokerage; the 2x stress must stay net-positive (V7).
- **Cost-sensitivity statement.** Short-horizon mean reversion turns its book over many times a year, so costs and tax are first-order, not a rounding. The gate series is net of every cost, slippage and tax; the frictionless (gross) version is reported beside it but never gated. A net-of-cost, net-of-tax edge is what is tested here; a gross edge alone is not a finding.
- Tax: `TaxModel::vda_statutory()` with `with_tds()` and `with_gst_on_fees()`: 30% + 4% cess on the fee-free gain of each winning trade, **no loss offset, fees not deductible**, 1% TDS on sells as a cash flow, GST on fees; open positions at the window end taxed as if sold. **Tax asymmetry warning (master 7.1):** an active mean-reversion book pays 34.2% on every winning trade but never recovers losing trades, so a high-turnover crypto book can be net-negative after tax with a positive pre-tax edge; a KILL must not be read as "no edge", and the pre-tax and after-tax numbers are both reported. Funding: Spot only, no funding.
- **Total-return basis of every P&L series; no price-only index as P&L:** confirmed.
- INR and USD dual reporting: Daily net returns in INR = (1 + r_USDT)(1 + r_USDINR) - 1 with `USDINR` matched by date (moves above 3% filtered, master 7.2); INR and USD both reported; for this unhedged long-only spot book the less favourable of the two is also shown but INR is the gating series.

## 10. Assumptions and ambiguities of the master resolved here (conservative choice; the owner or reviewer may only tighten)
- A1. The master gives no stop. The engine requires one per order (sizing). Fixed: 2 x ATR(14), the trend-pullback lineage's value, identical in every cell.
- A2. Max open positions 10 (master: declared per candidate, <= 20).
- A3. The SMA200 filter uses the instrument's own close (not a market index); the exit SMA(5) likewise.
- A4. Strict inequalities in every trigger; z-score uses the population standard deviation of closes including the current bar; RSI(2) is Wilder-smoothed with a simple-mean seed.
- A5. `hold` counts closed bars since the entry fill, the fill bar counting as bar 1; the exit then fills at the next open, so a position held at most `hold` sessions after the entry open.
- A6. Re-entry in the same symbol is allowed on any later bar; a symbol is entered at most once at a time.
- A7. Slippage: 10 bps per side adverse slippage (the engine takes one flat value per run: the conservative top-12 figure of master 7.2, 5 bps BTC/ETH and 10 bps other top-12 pairs; applying 10 to BTC/ETH too is the conservative choice).
- A8. Crypto dollar volume is `close * base volume` (quote volume is not in the store); month boundaries and 90-day windows are UTC calendar dates; a pair listed for fewer than 365 days at the month start is ineligible; a pair that leaves the top-12 keeps its open position to the normal exit.
- Long-only limitation: the engine has no short leg, so only the oversold (buy) side of mean reversion is tested; the overbought (short) side is not run and no claim is made about it.

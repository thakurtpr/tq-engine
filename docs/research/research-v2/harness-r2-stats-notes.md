## Ambiguities and gaps (none resolved by changing a threshold)

* **A-HAC-1.** The prereg says the engine's `tstats.rs` "MUST be fixed". It is fixed ONLY by adding `gate_t_v3` / `protocol_lag_v3` / `GateTV3`; the v2 `gate_t` / `protocol_lag` keep the holding-period floor so published outputs stay byte-identical. Callers on the research-v2 path must use the v3 function (the harness does).
* **A-DSR-1.** The prereg 4.3 daily rows reproduce exactly (kurtosis 9; 1.59 / 1.73 / 1.82 / 1.85, 1.45, 2.10; independent Python check). Its MONTHLY row (1.92 / 1.95) only reproduces at monthly kurtosis about 4, not 8-10. The harness computes the required Sharpe from each series' own skew and kurtosis, so no number is imposed.
* **A-DSR-2.** `sr_var` is the sample variance (ddof 1) of the per-period Sharpes of the class cells supplied by the caller (as the ledger does); which cells and the periodicity (daily vs monthly Sharpes) are the caller's responsibility, `VarianceSet` carries both. With fewer than 2 cells the 1/T floor applies.
* **A-SPA-1.** IG1 says "Hansen's SPA ... for the candidate's OWN base cell ... model universe = all counted cells of the class". A single-model SPA has no universe, and the universe-wide SPA p can be small because of another cell. The gating p is therefore the candidate's own studentized statistic against the bootstrap distribution of the MAX (floored at 0) over the universe with Hansen's consistent recentring (single-step adjusted p; equals the universe SPA p when the candidate is the universe's best). Reported beside it: the universe SPA (lower, consistent, upper) and White's RC p. A review may prefer the universe-wide SPA p as the gate; both are in the detail line.
* **A-SPA-2.** Ties count against the candidate (`>=`). Zero-variance models contribute nothing and get t = 0.
* **A-NEFF-1.** If the universe has >= 2 instruments but NO pair has 60 overlapping days, rho_bar is set to 1 (conservative) and flagged; a single-instrument universe has DE = 1. A pair whose correlation is undefined (constant series) is skipped.
* **A-WEEK-1.** "Friday daily bar is used where only a 00:00 UTC bar exists" is implemented as: the last price stamped at or before Friday 10:00 UTC (so the Friday 00:00 UTC bar, staleness 10 h) and a final week is emitted while the series ends within 15.5 h of its cut. From DAILY return series (candidates) the reported mismatch is a fixed 0 h (NSE) / 14 h (crypto); partial first and last weeks are dropped.
* **A-OG4-1.** "Partial years >= 6 months count" is implemented as >= 6 distinct calendar months with an observation. If the cumulative net log return is not positive, the 50% single-year concentration test cannot pass.
* **A-OG4-2.** Regime exposure share = fraction of the regime's days with positive exposure; the flat rule (mean >= 0) applies below 10%.
* **A-V5-1.** Sharpe uses sqrt(252) for every class including crypto (a 365-day series annualised with sqrt(252) is the stricter convention). V5 credits idle cash at `cash_rate/252 x (1 - exposure)` per day and uses Sharpe at rf = cash rate; sensitivity at 4% and 8% is in the detail.
* **A-V5-2.** V3 4c-rel uses the cost-and-tax-paying class benchmark return as the regressor (v3 says frictionless). The difference is a few bps a year.
* **A-V9-1.** The prereg fixes 10,000 runs but no seed for V9; 20261118 is used and the seed is a config field. "P(kill switch within 2 years)" is measured on the first 504 observations of each resampled path.
* **A-V10-1.** "No improvement beyond noise" for the shift test is not defined; one standard error of an annualised Sharpe, sqrt((1 + SR^2/2) / years), is used. This is a judgement call a reviewer may tighten.
* **A-BENCH-1.** Benchmark equity is on a liquidation basis (cash + net sale proceeds of every open lot - all tax due, realised and on a hypothetical sale that day). Equity tax nets by financial year through `TaxModel::equity_fy_tax`; crypto tax is per sale without loss offset; TDS is a cash lock reported separately, not P&L. B3 sleeves are each on their own venue's costs and tax.
* **A-BENCH-2.** The BTC chain needs the BTCUSDT-only return of 2017-08-17 as an INPUT (`splice_day_return`): the first BTCUSDT bar has no previous close, and the prereg does not say how that one return is formed.
* **A-OG2-1.** The one-sided 90% lower bound is the ceil(0.10 x draws)-th smallest bootstrap difference (conservative). An absent benchmark makes that OG2 line NOT APPLICABLE; an insufficient overlap (< 8 weeks or < 2 months) is NOT EVALUATED.
* **GAP-1 (by design, this is the statistics half).** The harness does NOT run strategies, walk-forward, the neighbour grid, random-priority seeds, 2x-cost reruns, random-entry baselines, the shift test or the prefix-invariance run itself. Their OUTCOMES are inputs (`Robustness`, `priority_seed_cagrs`, `Integrity`); a missing outcome is NOT EVALUATED and the verdict is INCOMPLETE, never a promotion. Caps, open-gate, single ledger writer and the holdout guard (prereg section 10 items 1-5, 7) are the other half of the harness and are not in this crate.
* **GAP-2.** The per-trade month-clustered t (4c-x for absolute books) is an optional input (`per_trade_clustered_t`); it is not computed here because trades carry no dates in this crate's input.
* **GAP-3.** OG6's "V5 evaluated cash-free for BOTH" and V5 are implemented on the supplied daily returns; the unrealised-at-end tax of the CANDIDATE is the caller's responsibility (the candidate series must already be liquidation-taxed).

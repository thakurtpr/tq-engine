## 2. Hypothesis and null
- H1: Crypto assets that outperformed over the formation window keep outperforming over the next month (under-reaction, retail attention and limits to arbitrage); a long-only top-bucket rotation (winners leg only), equal weight, monthly, earns a positive net return after taker fees, slippage and VDA tax, and beats BTC buy-and-hold (B2), the Nifty ETF (B1) and the 50/50 blend (B3) after deflating for N_true. Finance 77(2) for momentum in crypto returns. Honest note: the crypto paper's momentum signals use shorter lags (about 1 to 4 weeks) than this grid's L in {60, 90, 120}; the master fixed the grid, so the base cell is "design from J-T, asset from LTW" and is not claimed to be a replication.
- Null: net of costs and tax the top-k winners are no better than buy-and-hold BTC; any development winner among the 27 cells is selection. Known weakness: the universe is survivor-selected (below); the pool is small, so the top bucket is concentrated and highly correlated (large design effect, small N_eff); the VDA no-loss-offset tax punishes a rotating book on every winning sale and never credits a losing one; a monthly book on 12 to 20 names cannot reach 100 effective paper trades quickly.

## 4. Signal, sizing, execution (detailed enough to re-implement)
- **Calendar.** Decision date t = the last day of each calendar month (UTC daily bars); data up to the close of t only; targets fill at the next day's open (rebalancing engine path).
- **Score.** For a pair with daily closes C(0..n), n = the index of t: `score = C(n - s) / C(n - L) - 1`. Parameters: L = lookback days, s = skip days, k = names held. Eligible to be ranked: has a bar on t, at least max(L + 1, 366) closes, and `C(n - L) > 0`. No other filter.
- **Selection and weights.** Rank by score descending, ties by symbol ascending. Hold the top k at **equal weight 1/k**; fewer than k eligible: hold those at 1/k, rest in cash (cash earns 0, no USDT yield). Everything else is sold. One rule, one parameter vector for every instrument; no per-instrument tuning: **yes**.
- **Sizing (fixed here; drawdown-only failure is KILL).** Weight-based (the rebalancing engine ignores per-trade risk and sets no stops). Max open positions **8** (= largest k). **Notional cap per position 20% of equity (the master's assumption, unchanged; assumptions may only tighten).** k = 5 gives 19.9% after the 0.5% buffer and k = 8 gives 12.4%, both within the cap. **k = 3 would need 33% per name; the engine rejects any buy above the cap, so the three k = 3 cells hold zero exposure: degenerate cells, zero exposure under the 20% cap, counted in N, reported as such, cannot be profitable cells for OG5/ring-1 purposes.** K stays 27. The base cell (90, 7, 5) is unaffected (5 x 20% = 100%); of its 6 ring-1 neighbours (90, 7, 3) is degenerate. Gross <= 1, no leverage, spot only, no funding.
- **Kill-switch limit: ASSUMPTION 30%** drawdown; OFF in the assessment run, ON in the operating run.
- Priority among simultaneous buys: alphabetical in the base run, >= 20 random-priority seeds, median judged, 5th percentile net CAGR > 0 (V1).
- Declared 4c type: **benchmark-relative** (invested share is high once >= k pairs are eligible; the book is near fully invested and beta to BTC is expected >= 0.5); alpha t at 3.0 against BTC (B2); IG1 benchmark B2. Declared before any result; the stricter reading.

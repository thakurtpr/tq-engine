## A3.2 As-run exit semantics (the executable spec governs)
The registered candidate is the committed spec JSON run by the one engine used for all 14 candidates. The prose of several candidate preregs (o2-c1..o2-c4, o2-c8 and others) says positions are "held to expiry (registry defaults)". That was a wrong description of the defaults: the engine default exit is `exit_before_expiry_days = 1`, i.e. the position is closed at the open of the session before expiry (exit slippage on all legs), unless the spec sets `hold_to_expiry`. As run: o2-c13 holds to expiry; o2-c9, o2-c10, o2-c11 set `exit_before_expiry_days = 1`; o2-c12 has `exit_before_expiry_days` as its axis (1, 2, 3); o2-c1..c8 and o2-c17 use the default (close one session before expiry). **Where a candidate prereg prose says "held to expiry" this addendum supersedes it.** Consequence, stated plainly: the exit slippage on near-worthless legs biases a structure's net result toward loss, so the KILL verdicts are not too generous, but **hold-to-expiry variants of o2-c1..c8 were not tested**: they would be new hypotheses (new candidates need cap slots that do not exist) and the 11-cell reserve is for bug re-runs only.

## A3.3 Expiry class rule (disclosed engine behaviour)
The last listed expiry of each month is classed Monthly, so a Weekly policy skips it and the next cycle's target expiry is more than 8 days ahead; positions then overlap on one expiry and the risk that week is up to doubled. This applies to every weekly candidate (o2-c1..c4, c7, c8, c13, c17) identically. Not changed; disclosed.

## A3.4 o2-c8
The first run of o2-c8 was refused when the account of its first cell (far_width_ratio 1: a net-debit long put butterfly) went to about -51,145 on a 10,000,000 account on 2022-11-29. Diagnosis (mechanism only, on development data cut at 2025-04-30; S3 never read): not a sizing, builder, direction, quantity or settlement defect (the engine's booked P&L replays to the paisa); a genuine consecutive-loss path: the debit structure loses roughly its debit in most weeks and, with entry and exit slippage, about 2-4% of the account per cycle against a 3% sizing budget. A ruined cell is a KILL (A2.11, G8-physical). The refused sweep `fb88248d...` stays counted and is declared an orphan (no cell ran, nothing stored); the re-run under defect 4's fix uses the reserve (3 cells), with a committed reserve addendum, and only completes the record: its verdict is already determined by the disclosed ruin.
